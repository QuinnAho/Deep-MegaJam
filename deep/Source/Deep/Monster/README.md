# Monster: the sea serpent

**How it works.** `UDeepMonsterBrain` (world subsystem, server) spawns one `ADeepSerpent` at stop 2 and deeper (`FirstStop`), 45 m+ out in the dark. Each diver's `UDeepRunComponent` reports its noise (winching, sprint speed, grabs) through `UDeepMonsterRules::ReportDiverNoise`, which goes into Unreal's hearing sense (`UAISense_Hearing`). Dead, settling and descending divers are not prey. The serpent is a Pawn possessed by `ADeepSerpentController` (an `AAIController`). The controller hears through a `UAIPerceptionComponent`, tests the lamp cone against the head, calls the pure `UDeepMonsterRules::NextState` and steers the pawn with its own turn-rate steering (open water: no navmesh, no `MoveTo`). States: Lurk (circles beyond lamp range) -> Stalk (noise >= 60: spirals in on the middle of the tether) -> Bite (5 s alarm, then severed) or Recoil (beam held 0.75 s on its head, or any beam during a bite) -> cooldown -> Lurk. Hunt only happens when the diver has no tether. Silence always calms it: noise decays 10/s.
**The first sight (state Pass, third pass).** Once per run, before its first stalk or hunt, it shows itself: when noise would first bring it in, the diver loses the tether, or `FirstSightAfterS` (20 s) of lurking passes, it enters Pass instead. The run's `monster_first_heard` plays first (the controller broadcasts `OnStalkStarted`, so the Captain's first line moves to the pass and the first real stalk gets `monster_near`). The head is placed out of view and out of the beam, 18 m to one side of the lamp's view, then swims slowly (3 m/s) across it at 7 m, turns away at the beam's axis and leaves sideways into the dark, so the 16 m body drags through the beam behind it. Noise and the tether do not interrupt it; the beam held on its head for `PassLitToRecoilS` (5 s) does. Then Lurk and the normal rules. Only for a player-controlled diver and the brain's own spawn: tests and cheat spawns keep the old rules exactly. `Deep.Monster.FirstSight` runs it now. Numbers: `Behaviour` "First sight". Tests: `Deep.Sim.Monster.FirstSight`, `Deep.World.Monster.FirstSight`.
**Staged bite and recoil.** Stalk now aims at a point `BiteFromDiverM` (4 m) up the line from the diver; inside `StalkDirectWithinM` it swings out `BiteSideLeadM` (3 m) to one side of the line and strikes across it, so a diver who turns sees it in profile on the line. Recoil first turns the head away from the lamp nearly in place for `RecoilTurnS` (0.9 s, in frame), then flees as before. Shot: `Deep.Monster.StageBite [side_m]` turns the view to the bite point and sends the head in from the right.
**The well (the last ascent).** The relic's shaft (`UDeepMonsterBrain::GetWell`: the bed's sinkhole, radius `Look.SeabedPitRadiusM`, lined with stone that has no collision) hides everything outside it, so while the focus diver is in it every steering target is moved inside (`UDeepMonsterRules::WellAimPoint`, `KeepInWell`, `Behaviour` "Well"): Lurk circles the axis below the diver at `WellHoldBelowM` (deep enough that a stalk from there, closing at stalk speed less the diver's rise, takes the open-water time: about 20 m while winching at stop 3), Stalk comes straight up from below to the diver's end of the line, Recoil turns down and dives for the floor. It enters the shaft once the diver is `WellEnterAboveFloorM` up, placed below him out of view if it was in the rock. Rules, speeds and timers are unchanged. `Deep.Monster.Well 0` = old steering; `Deep.Monster.WellLog 1` logs `DEEPWELL {json}`; `Deep.Monster.Watch 1|2|3` aims the view at the head, straight down, or like the scripted player. Test: `Deep.Sim.Monster.Well`.
**The look** (`ADeepSerpent`, Style Guide section 6; numbers in `Body`): one procedural tube (`UProceduralMeshComponent`, 48 rings x 12, rebuilt each frame along the head's recorded path with parallel-transport frames, a dorsal ridge, a travelling weave that banks into curves), a code-built wedge head with a hinged jaw on `HeadSocket`, skin `M_SerpentSkin` (clear coat over near-black, round overlapping scales in the normal, rough grooves) and markings layer `M_SerpentMarks` (sparse photophores low on the flanks, no outline: rim gain 0), both built from text by `Content/Deep/Serpent/build_serpent_skin.py`, headless. Second pass: 16 m long, 1.1 to 1.4 m thick, a long flat head with teeth, small eyes. The controller sends `SetMood(state, threat)`: spots stay dark until threat peaks, then pulse faster, the head flinches on Recoil and lunges and thrashes on Bite. Eyes shine only while a diver's lamp is on them. Markings stay dark for `RevealDelayS` and until it first stalks (`Deep.Monster.Reveal` skips that). `bTubeBody = false` brings back the old spheres. Test: `Deep.World.Monster.Look`.

**The dragon head** (`Body.MeshHead`, Nov 15): when the Dragon Cave pack is installed the serpent wears `SM_DragonPose04` (a whole 250 m winged dragon, mouth open) scaled 0.075 in the head socket. Its landmarks (skull point, eyes, neck cut, all mesh-local cm) place it: the skull is the socket, so `GetHeadLocation()` (now the socket, which leads in a lunge) is the visible head; the eye line levels it; the eyeshine sits on its eyes. `M_DragonHead` clips everything outside a capsule in front of the neck cut (no wings, no legs) and grades the dragon's textures to near-black wet skin. The body wears `M_DragonBody`: a patch of the dragon's own neck skin, mirror-tiled, so the scales at the join are the same scales. The tube starts as a sleeve inside the neck and is just over it by the cut (tall and narrow there like the neck, round by 4 m), with dorsal spikes carried on down the ridge. The bite (it cannot close its jaw): the mouth landmark is held on the line, the head turns side-on to the diver and worries the line (`MeshHead.BiteShake*`), look only. The drawn body keeps `Body.BodyClearM` (2 m) from every diver's eyes; hit segments keep the true path. The lamp asks `GetDrawnSpheres()` where the drawn body is (one sphere per tube ring and three along the head) and dims for it: the serpent stays without collision. Both materials: `Content/Deep/Serpent/build_dragon_head.py`, headless. No pack = the code-built head, every test the same. `Deep.Monster.MeshHead 0|1` switches it at run time. Look-dev: `Deep.Monster.Specimens [cell_m] [clip] [lux]` (the four poses as a contact sheet), `Deep.Monster.PlainLight <lux>`, `Deep.Monster.Param <head|body> <name> <x> [y z]`. Cook: listed in `Content/Data/PackAssets.csv`.

**Settings that matter most for feel** (Project Settings > Game > DEEP Monster, `UDeepMonsterSettings`): `StalkNoise`/`CalmNoise`/`NoiseDecayPerS` (how quickly it comes and goes), `WinchNoisePerS`/`SprintNoisePerS`/`GrabNoise`, `LurkRadiusM` (keep it above the lamp's 35 m), `LitToRecoilS` and `BeamLeniencyM` (how forgiving the lamp is), `BiteAlarmS`, and per stop `Stops[]` (`RecoilCooldownS`, `StalkSpeedMps`, `HuntSpeedMps`). Stop 3 is meaner only through `Stops[]`.

**Wires (connected, in `UDeepRunComponent`; all events fire on the server and name the diver):**
1. Noise: `StepMonsterWires` reports winch and sprint each step; `NotifyPickup` reports a grab. The brain no longer watches divers itself (no double count). Anything else loud can call `ReportDiverNoise` too (60 brings it in).
2. Bite to the cut: `OnBiteStarted` -> replicated alarm (`GetBiteAlarmLeft`, HUD band) + `bite_alarm`; `OnBiteBroken`/`OnRepelled` clear it (+ `monster_repelled`); `OnTetherSevered` -> `CutTetherFor(cut_bite)`, the same Cut as Patience, refused if no alarm was up. `SetState` calls `NotifyTetherCut`/`NotifyRetethered` on every cut, death, re-tether and respawn.
3. Caught to death: `OnDiverCaught` -> `Die(true)` (`death_monster`, "Taken by the serpent" screen), then the normal respawn.
4. Threat: per-diver `GetMonsterThreat01` (replicated) drives a red edge pulse and a SOMETHING NEAR bar. `OnStalkStarted` -> `monster_first_heard` once per run, then `monster_near` (also when threat passes 0.6). Patience drains x2 while it stalks or bites you.
Sound is not wired (no sound assets yet): hook music/heartbeat to `GetMonsterThreat01`.

**Inspect it.** In PIE press `'` (apostrophe) for the gameplay debugger, look at the serpent, then use numpad 4 for the Perception category to see heard noise events and its hearing range. `Deep.Monster.State` logs `DEEPMONSTER {json}`. Other cheats: `Deep.Monster.Spawn`, `Deep.Monster.Place <m> [deg] [right_m] [up_m]` (head in front of the view, or off the beam), `Deep.Monster.Pose <state|-1>` (shots: show a state's motion, brain untouched), `Deep.Monster.Noise <n>`, `Deep.Monster.Lit <s>`, `Deep.Lamp <0|1>`. Bite alarm shot: `.\tools\ue.ps1 shot -Name bite -Cmds "Deep.GotoStop 2;wait 1;Deep.Lamp 0;Deep.Look 35;Deep.Monster.Place 14 90;Deep.Monster.Noise 95;wait 6.5"`.
Each state is a separate branch of `NextState` and a separate case in `ADeepSerpentController::Steer`, so the five states could be moved into a StateTree later without changing the rules or their tests.
