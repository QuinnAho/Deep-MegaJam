# Monster: the sea serpent

**How it works.** `UDeepMonsterBrain` (world subsystem, server) spawns one `ADeepSerpent` at stop 2 and deeper (`FirstStop`), 45 m+ out in the dark. It watches each diver (winching, sprint speed, any cargo rise) and reports that noise through `UDeepMonsterRules::ReportDiverNoise`, which goes into Unreal's hearing sense (`UAISense_Hearing`). The serpent is a Pawn possessed by `ADeepSerpentController` (an `AAIController`). The controller hears through a `UAIPerceptionComponent`, tests the lamp cone against the head, calls the pure `UDeepMonsterRules::NextState` and steers the pawn with its own turn-rate steering (open water: no navmesh, no `MoveTo`). States: Lurk (circles beyond lamp range) -> Stalk (noise >= 60: spirals in on the middle of the tether) -> Bite (5 s alarm, then severed) or Recoil (beam held 0.75 s on its head, or any beam during a bite) -> cooldown -> Lurk. Hunt only happens when the diver has no tether. Silence always calms it: noise decays 10/s.
The body has no assets: about 20 instanced spheres follow the head's recorded path, with a travelling weave and two unlit glowing eyes.

**Settings that matter most for feel** (Project Settings > Game > DEEP Monster, `UDeepMonsterSettings`): `StalkNoise`/`CalmNoise`/`NoiseDecayPerS` (how quickly it comes and goes), `WinchNoisePerS`/`SprintNoisePerS`/`GrabNoise`, `LurkRadiusM` (keep it above the lamp's 35 m), `LitToRecoilS` and `BeamLeniencyM` (how forgiving the lamp is), `BiteAlarmS`, and per stop `Stops[]` (`RecoilCooldownS`, `StalkSpeedMps`, `HuntSpeedMps`). Stop 3 is meaner only through `Stops[]`.

**Wires to connect after the merge** (all events fire on the server and name the diver):
1. Noise sources: anything loud calls `UDeepMonsterRules::ReportDiverNoise(World, Location, Loudness, Instigator)` (loudness in noise units, 60 brings it in). The brain's own diver watching can stay or be removed once real sources exist.
2. Bite to the cut: `OnBiteStarted(Diver, Seconds)` starts the alarm UI/sound, `OnBiteBroken(Diver)` stops it, `OnTetherSevered(Diver)` runs the cut. Whatever cuts or restores a tether calls `Brain->NotifyTetherCut(Diver)` / `NotifyRetethered(Diver)` (re-tether, respawn), or the serpent never knows the diver is free or safe again.
3. Caught to death: `OnDiverCaught(Diver)` kills that diver (then call `NotifyRetethered` on respawn).
4. Threat to sound and HUD: `Brain->GetThreat01()` (0 far, 1 on top of you or biting) drives music/breathing/heartbeat and any HUD pulse. `GetState()` names the state for subtitles.

**Inspect it.** In PIE press `'` (apostrophe) for the gameplay debugger, look at the serpent, then use numpad 4 for the Perception category to see heard noise events and its hearing range. `Deep.Monster.State` logs `DEEPMONSTER {json}`. Other cheats: `Deep.Monster.Spawn`, `Deep.Monster.Noise <n>`, `Deep.Monster.Lit <s>`.
Each state is a separate branch of `NextState` and a separate case in `ADeepSerpentController::Steer`, so the five states could be moved into a StateTree later without changing the rules or their tests.
