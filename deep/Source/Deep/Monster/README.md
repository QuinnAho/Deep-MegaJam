# Monster: the sea serpent

**How it works.** `UDeepMonsterBrain` (world subsystem, server) spawns one `ADeepSerpent` at stop 2 and deeper (`FirstStop`), 45 m+ out in the dark. Each diver's `UDeepRunComponent` reports its noise (winching, sprint speed, grabs) through `UDeepMonsterRules::ReportDiverNoise`, which goes into Unreal's hearing sense (`UAISense_Hearing`). Dead, settling and descending divers are not prey. The serpent is a Pawn possessed by `ADeepSerpentController` (an `AAIController`). The controller hears through a `UAIPerceptionComponent`, tests the lamp cone against the head, calls the pure `UDeepMonsterRules::NextState` and steers the pawn with its own turn-rate steering (open water: no navmesh, no `MoveTo`). States: Lurk (circles beyond lamp range) -> Stalk (noise >= 60: spirals in on the middle of the tether) -> Bite (5 s alarm, then severed) or Recoil (beam held 0.75 s on its head, or any beam during a bite) -> cooldown -> Lurk. Hunt only happens when the diver has no tether. Silence always calms it: noise decays 10/s.
**The look** (`ADeepSerpent`, Style Guide section 6; numbers in `Body`): one procedural tube (`UProceduralMeshComponent`, 48 rings x 12, rebuilt each frame along the head's recorded path with parallel-transport frames, a dorsal ridge, a travelling weave that banks into curves), a code-built wedge head with a hinged jaw on `HeadSocket` (set `Body.HeadMesh` to drop in a static mesh head there; eyes and markings stay on top), skin `M_SerpentSkin` and markings layer `M_SerpentMarks` (built from text by `Content/Deep/Serpent/build_serpent_skin.py`, headless). The controller sends `SetMood(state, threat)`: spots pulse faster with threat, the head flinches on Recoil and lunges and thrashes on Bite. Eyes shine only while a diver's lamp is on them. Markings stay dark for `RevealDelayS` and until it first stalks (`Deep.Monster.Reveal` skips that). `bTubeBody = false` brings back the old spheres. Test: `Deep.World.Monster.Look`.

**Settings that matter most for feel** (Project Settings > Game > DEEP Monster, `UDeepMonsterSettings`): `StalkNoise`/`CalmNoise`/`NoiseDecayPerS` (how quickly it comes and goes), `WinchNoisePerS`/`SprintNoisePerS`/`GrabNoise`, `LurkRadiusM` (keep it above the lamp's 35 m), `LitToRecoilS` and `BeamLeniencyM` (how forgiving the lamp is), `BiteAlarmS`, and per stop `Stops[]` (`RecoilCooldownS`, `StalkSpeedMps`, `HuntSpeedMps`). Stop 3 is meaner only through `Stops[]`.

**Wires (connected, in `UDeepRunComponent`; all events fire on the server and name the diver):**
1. Noise: `StepMonsterWires` reports winch and sprint each step; `NotifyPickup` reports a grab. The brain no longer watches divers itself (no double count). Anything else loud can call `ReportDiverNoise` too (60 brings it in).
2. Bite to the cut: `OnBiteStarted` -> replicated alarm (`GetBiteAlarmLeft`, HUD band) + `bite_alarm`; `OnBiteBroken`/`OnRepelled` clear it (+ `monster_repelled`); `OnTetherSevered` -> `CutTetherFor(cut_bite)`, the same Cut as Patience, refused if no alarm was up. `SetState` calls `NotifyTetherCut`/`NotifyRetethered` on every cut, death, re-tether and respawn.
3. Caught to death: `OnDiverCaught` -> `Die(true)` (`death_monster`, "Taken by the serpent" screen), then the normal respawn.
4. Threat: per-diver `GetMonsterThreat01` (replicated) drives a red edge pulse and a SOMETHING NEAR bar. `OnStalkStarted` -> `monster_first_heard` once per run, then `monster_near` (also when threat passes 0.6). Patience drains x2 while it stalks or bites you.
Sound is not wired (no sound assets yet): hook music/heartbeat to `GetMonsterThreat01`.

**Inspect it.** In PIE press `'` (apostrophe) for the gameplay debugger, look at the serpent, then use numpad 4 for the Perception category to see heard noise events and its hearing range. `Deep.Monster.State` logs `DEEPMONSTER {json}`. Other cheats: `Deep.Monster.Spawn`, `Deep.Monster.Place <m> [deg]` (head in front of the view), `Deep.Monster.Noise <n>`, `Deep.Monster.Lit <s>`, `Deep.Lamp <0|1>`. Bite alarm shot: `.\tools\ue.ps1 shot -Name bite -Cmds "Deep.GotoStop 2;wait 1;Deep.Lamp 0;Deep.Look 35;Deep.Monster.Place 14 90;Deep.Monster.Noise 95;wait 6.5"`.
Each state is a separate branch of `NextState` and a separate case in `ADeepSerpentController::Steer`, so the five states could be moved into a StateTree later without changing the rules or their tests.
