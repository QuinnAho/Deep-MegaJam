# Polish - Sub Assets (free submarine search)

Sketchfab links use the model id form `https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/<id>`; the id is the slug shown. All picks below are CC Attribution 4.0: shippable in a packaged game, with a credit line (author, title, link, licence) in the credits and the submission text. Sketchfab downloads give glTF/GLB (some also FBX) with PBR textures in a zip; import through Interchange. Triangle counts are the API `faceCount`. Everything needs the Style Guide section 4 grade (saturation 0.7, textures at most 2048, dark tint).

Honest finding: no free, realistic, ready-made small research sub with a worn finish was found. The best free options are fan or hobby models with unknown wear; one of them is a good base only if the textures are dark and grubby. Plan to retexture or darken.

## Top picks

1. **Atlantic Explorer Submarine** by JosephWPugsley. Replaces: the placeholder sub exterior and cabin.
   - https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/f1645a6ec3b54e81aed7b2c105cdd6e2 (GLB) and https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/9e9d1a462983469ebdca2a77f5249b12 (FBX)
   - CC-BY 4.0. About 120k tris, 64k verts, game-ready tag, textures partly Poly Haven CC0 and partly procedural Blender. Texture size and PBR not stated. Has an interior with passenger seating.
   - Fictional research sub (inspired by the film The Abyss, rebuilt from scratch). Wear unknown. Exterior plus interior in one file, the only such free candidate. At 120k tris it fits the game with no Nanite fuss.
   - Acceptance test: likely passes 1 and 3 after grading if the textures are muted; unknown until viewed. Check for saturated paint.

2. **BT-01 (Bester-1 deep-sea rescue bathyscaphe)** by Oliv1e. Replaces: the exterior hull seen by the diver and in the trailer.
   - https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/7eb3b5312ec044b08ffd2d87e7761cfc
   - CC-BY 4.0. About 437k tris (use Nanite or decimate), Substance Painter textured, hard-surface, author worked out an interior. Texture size and PBR not stated. 3,000 downloads, 696 likes.
   - A real small deep-sea rescue DSV, the right type and scale. Likely a clean-ish render model, so the wear has to come from our grade and dirt layers. A duplicate upload "Bathyscaphe DONE" by joejoe980 has the same face count; use the original.
   - Acceptance test: shape passes; finish probably fails 1 until dirtied.

3. **Beached Old Submarine PBR Scan** by perz_scans. Replaces: nothing directly. Source of real rust and barnacle-style hull texture, or a wreck set piece on the seabed.
   - https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/702b2386ddce4b71ab283095b4cbd52b
   - CC-BY 4.0. About 100k tris, photogrammetry, "PBR", noted as having artifacts. A real old sub hull (Pula, Croatia), so it is a military-style hull, not a research sub.
   - The only candidate that is certainly worn and photographed, which is the Style Guide's first line. Good as a wreck or for material sampling.
   - Acceptance test: passes 1 (if scan is dark), 3 after grading.

4. **Valve wheels** (props for ballast and hatch). Replaces: placeholder wheels and levers.
   - Rusty Industrial Valve Wheel by Poligonik, 3.4k tris, CC-BY: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/629d9deb3e4e4ac6af28c8136933a919
   - Pipe Wheel 3D scan by zdenkoroman, 20k tris, CC-BY: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/5b8d5bb5ecdd4d8a80f71eabd4d08249
   - Valve Wheel 3D scan by zdenkoroman, 30k tris, CC-BY: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/0264beb63c214817be93b57b23b6e6b3
   - Rusty by description or scanned, game-light. Acceptance test: pass after grading (rust may be too orange; desaturate).

5. **Gauges** (pressure and depth dials). Replaces: placeholder dials on the console.
   - Gauges by CaptainMorgan, 36 tris, tagged submarine, CC-BY: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/9da80bb1dcf64a63a8cf31b0d6cd3e43 (too low to look real up close; use as a face plate base)
   - Pressure gauge by takeboncog, 5.6k tris, CC-BY: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/95cf5b63b15549a2a173bb480577b6a9
   - The pressure gauge (antique, described as damaged), by WirtualneMuzeaMalopolski, 300k tris, **CC0** (no credit needed): https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/65c754a25e13457cbc45b2522fa0dba8 . Heavy, but a museum scan; the best-looking worn gauge, usable as a hero prop with Nanite.
   - Acceptance test: dials must glow amber only (section 3); the needles and faces are ours to retexture.

"Industrial VOL.3 - Consoles and Control Panels" (Dekogon) is probably paid. Check with the editor's Fab free filter for "submersible", "ROV", "bathyscaphe". ## Rejected

- OceanGate Titan models (several, CC-BY, 127k to 450k tris): a real, lost vessel with a fatal-accident history; poor taste for a horror trailer, and the shape is a tube with a plain white hull.
- Nautilus Submarine (SDC), Steampunk underwater explorer, Nautilus Prism: steampunk or Verne fantasy, clashes with realistic style.
- Yellow Submarine (6.5k tris), The Yellow Submarine (11M tris), Jacques Cousteau's yellow submarine: saturated yellow paint, forbidden by section 2; cartoon or too heavy.
- Bathyscaphe Cartoon, Bathyscaphe (Sosiska555, 4k tris): cartoon or too coarse.
- Typhoon Class, Kilo Class, Type 209, Samidare: military warships; the brief excludes them.
- Subnautica Hydra (CC-BY-NC), Submarine Dials and Gauge Cluster (jonathanboring, CC-BY-NC-SA), Submarine Engine (CC-BY-NC-ND): non-commercial or no-derivatives licences; do not ship.
- Pipeline Pressure Gauge (s.wdwd): AI-generated Meshy mesh at 427k tris; uneven quality.
- Full-interior mixmamo.studio carriers (1.8M to 4.7M tris): far too heavy and a different kind of ship.
- Poly Haven: no sub or valve models; useful only for textures (CC0).
