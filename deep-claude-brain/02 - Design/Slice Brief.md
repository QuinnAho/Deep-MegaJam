# Slice Brief

## What the slice is
The first ten minutes of DEEP, start to finish, in a real map built in the editor. It is the bar for everything after: if the slice is not fun and frightening, more content will not fix it. We judge it by playing it.

## Pillars (draft)
Taken from what Quinn has said since Saturday. Every feature has to serve at least one of them.
1. **Drive the sub through the dark.** Real controls (throttle, ballast, rudder) in tight caverns and tunnels, and the hull pays for every bump. Quinn: "driving the sub ... is part of it".
2. **Hunted by sound.** The serpent is heard before it is seen. It taunts the player, and it hears the player too. Quinn: "the monster needs to be taunting the player ... make sure you can hear it and it can hear you".
3. **Immersion first.** Information lives in the world (gauges, levers, the camera feed, sound), not in a cluttered HUD. Quinn: "the UI ... really takes away from the immersion".
4. **Seen through a camera.** The outside is seen through the sub's camera and the diver's lamp, and the picture itself reacts: grain, and interference near the serpent (D-041).

| HUD | **Bare**: a reticle and prompts only | Quinn's call on clutter. The HUD pass comes later, done once (D-028). |

## What Quinn blocks out
