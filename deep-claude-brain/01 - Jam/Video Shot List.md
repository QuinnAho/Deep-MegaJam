# Video Shot List

Goal: 30 to 60 seconds, recorded by hand by Quinn in OBS on the 6:45 PM freeze package, at about 7:10 PM. Judges must see the theme, the loop and the look with no explanation. Strongest first. Aim for 50 s.

## Keys

- Helm: W/S step the six-notch throttle (one notch per press). A/D rudder. Hold C to flood, hold Space to blow; the ballast lever stays where you leave it. X all stop. E stand up.
- Water: WASD swim, Space/C up and down, E grab, left mouse harpoon (one per dive), F lamp (once bought), V sonar ping, T unclip, R hold to winch home.

Do one rehearsal run first. Shots can come from separate takes. Shot 5 is the hardest to get on demand. The Captain is silent by default, so there is no radio line or subtitle to show.

**Before recording:** the lamp (F) works only after the flashlight is bought at the first settlement, and shots 4 and 5 need it. The package is a Development build, so the console (the ` key) and its cheats should work: `Deep.BuyGate free` buys the flashlight and `Deep.GotoStop 2` jumps to stop 2. Try them once before recording. If the console does not open, play one dive and buy the flashlight by hand. `Deep.Monster.Taunt` and `Deep.Unclip` exist as cheats, if the serpent or T misbehave.

## Avoid

- The title menu, settings and pause menu. Start recording in play.
- Dead time: long swims, waiting for the serpent. Cut or skip.
- Mouse spinning. Turn slowly; the look is the point.
- Any frame with a notification, the taskbar, or a second monitor.
- Dying early in the take. Re-take.
- An unsteered descent. Holding C with no rudder wrecks the hull on the bends. Fly the bends.
- Hitting V at the helm: the ping is the diver's, in the water.

## OBS settings

- Window Capture or Game Capture on the packaged `Deep.exe`; canvas and output 1920x1080, 60 fps.
- Recording: MP4, NVENC H.264, CQP 18 (or 20 Mbps CBR). Keep it under 100 MB for upload speed.
- Audio: Desktop audio only, mic muted. Check the meter moves on a ping.
