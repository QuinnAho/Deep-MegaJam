# Partner Perks

The redemption codes and links are in the jam email of Nov 13 2026 in Quinn's inbox. **They are deliberately not copied here**: the email asks jammers not to share them, and this repo may be public. Each perk is one per person, so Colby claims his own.

## Worth claiming for this game

| Perk | What we would use it for | Claim by | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tripo** (3,000 credits, 7 days of Pro) | Text or image to 3D: salvage props, the submarine, the monster. | Redeem by **Nov 16** | Fastest route to custom meshes. |
| **Hyper3D Rodin** (Business tier, 208 credits) | One hero asset, such as the relic. | Redeem by **Nov 16** | Using Rodin and listing it makes a winning team eligible for a share of a $10,000 pool. |
| **Meshy** (one month Pro, 1,000 credits) | Text to 3D with texturing and rigging. | Sign up by Nov 17 | Needs a credit card and auto-renews. Cancel after the jam. |
| **PBRgen** | Tileable PBR materials: hull metal, rock, silt. | Valid to **Nov 15** | |
| **WeLoveIndies** | Sound effects and music. | Open | Free for non-commercial use. A commercial release needs a paid license, so track every file used. |
| **fal** ($30 credit) | Image, audio or 3D generation through one API. | Claim by Nov 30 | Backup for voice or textures. |

## Lower priority

- **Diversion:** one free month of cloud version control built for Unreal binaries. Only worth switching if Git LFS quota becomes a problem. Redeem by Nov 22.
- **SpaceXSI:** $50 across Cursor and Grok. Claim by **Nov 15**.
- **Reallusion iClone, Rokoko:** character animation and mocap. A first-person game with one creature does not need them in 24 hours.
- **NetApp and AWS:** a build-pipeline session after the jam.
- **Anthropic:** Claude API credits were all claimed.
