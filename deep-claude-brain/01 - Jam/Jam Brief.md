# Jam Brief

## The essentials

- **Event:** 2026 Epic AI MegaJam, hosted by Epic Games on itch.io. Use of AI is optional and unlimited.
- **Dates:** Nov 12 to Nov 15, 2026. Building could start at Nov 12, 12:00 AM EST.
- **Deadline:** **Nov 15, 5:59 PM PST / 8:59 PM EST.** Late submissions are disqualified. The "Nov 16" seen in some places is the same instant in UTC.
- **Theme:** **DIRECTOR'S CUT.** Epic's text: "Your vision, your edits, your final cut. Every developer has that one feature that didn't quite make it: the mechanic you scoped out at 2 AM, or the idea you shelved and never found a place for. Now's your chance to finally build it. Using AI agents? You're directing those too. Set the vision, delegate tasks, and decide what makes the cut."
- **Team size:** solo or up to five people. No limit on AI agents.
- **Who can enter:** age 18+, active Epic Games account.
- **Engine:** Unreal Engine or UEFN, not both. Any UE version for desktop. The 5.9 MegaJam Preview is only required for web builds.
- **Scale:** 10.3k joined and 155 entries when checked at about 8:20 PM EST on Nov 14.
- **Winners announced:** by Jan 29, 2027.

## Rules that affect how we build

- Do not build before Nov 12. Declare any prompts, scripts or assets made earlier. (We started Nov 14, so nothing to declare.)
- Assets we did not make (Fab and others) are allowed with the right license. List them in the submission.
- Gameplay must go beyond Epic's starter templates.
- If a tool, plugin, SDK or asset needs attribution, include it the way the rights owner asks.
- Keep track of the tools used. The "Tools you used" field decides some partner prizes.
- Entrants keep ownership of their game.

## Judging

A panel scores each qualified entry from 1 to 5 on seven criteria:

1. Ambition and innovation
2. Theme
3. Fun factor
4. Gameplay
5. Visuals
6. Level design and game progression
7. Audio

Weighting and tie-breaks are not published on the jam page. Online and in-person entries are judged the same way.

What this means for us: all seven count, so a build with no audio or no progression loses points that a small amount of work would earn. A judge needs to see the theme, the loop and the look within the first two minutes.

## Prizes

See [[Track Strategy]] for which ones we are going for.

- **Best Overall, Unreal Engine:** $7,500 / $5,000 / $2,500.
- **Best Student Project:** $7,500 / $5,000 / $2,500. "Must be currently enrolled at a higher-education institution." Whether every member must be enrolled is in the Official Rules, which we have not been able to read.
- **Best Team:** $5,000. Best game by a team of two or more.
- **Render Royalty:** $2,500. Best visuals, photoreal or stylized.
- **Render Royalty, NVIDIA RTX Award:** one GeForce RTX 5090 to the highest-ranked eligible Render Royalty entry built in Unreal Engine that uses NvRTX features, NVIDIA DLSS, or NVIDIA ACE.
- **OpenAI Awards (API credits):** $5,000 for the Best Overall UE entry that makes the best use of OpenAI models or products; $2,500 for the best Render Royalty entry that uses them "during development, gameplay, or both".
- **Glass Box:** $2,500 plus an ASUS ROG Ally per team member. Best dev log showing how the game was built.
- **Solo Driver, Pocket Powerhouse (mobile), Walkie-Talkie (UEFN):** not for us.
- **Development Continuation Grants:** $50,000 / $25,000 / $10,000 plus Fab credit for standout entries.
- **Partner extras:** Hyper3D splits a $10,000 cash pool among winning teams that used Rodin and listed it. Winners also get Rodin, Tripo and Meshy subscriptions.
